How do I get my page?
  1. Open your page in Chrome.
  2. Right-click, choose View page source, select everything and copy.
  3. Paste it above. Or press Ctrl+S to save the page and upload that file.

        A clean scan is not a compliance certificate

        Automated checks cover only part of the accessibility guidelines (WCAG). FixLoop never claims compliance: it shows what it fixed and proved, and what still needs a person using a keyboard and a screen reader. Your page never leaves your browser, and its scripts are removed before checking.
